# Interview guide — Creator-tech brand safety

**Domain expert:** Brad Webb
**GTM interviewer:** Titus Capilnean
**Time:** 45–60 min
**Goal:** Scope a creator-content brand-safety scoring agent for an influencer marketing platform or brand partnership team.

## Framing for Brad (read verbatim)

"Run this like a prospect call. You're the VP of Brand Partnerships at a mid-size influencer marketing platform — 150 brands, 12,000 creators in your network, running 400 campaigns a quarter across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. I'm Titus, a peer operator from the AI training-data and enterprise-AI side. Stay in role. Use your real publisher and adtech experience as the source material."

## Questions

### Pain
1. How many pieces of creator content do you review before a campaign goes live? Per week?
2. Who does the review today — account manager, brand safety analyst, the brand themselves?
3. What's the reject rate? What's the most common reason?
4. Last time a brand-safety incident hit post-publish, what happened? Who caught it, how fast?

### Current state
5. What does the review cover — just the deliverable (post/video), or also the creator's recent feed, audience demographics, adjacent content?
6. Brand guidelines — PDF per brand, a wiki, a checklist, tribal knowledge?
7. How many brands share similar safety rules vs. need fully bespoke ones?
8. Tooling today — Brandwatch, CreatorIQ, Grin, manual spreadsheets?

### Success
9. If the agent works, what changes first — speed, consistency, coverage, all three?
10. What's the minimum quality bar for an auto-approve? What confidence level would you trust?
11. What's the first failure that would make a brand pull spend?

### Brand-safety taxonomy
12. Walk me through your severity tiers — what's an instant reject vs. a flag-for-review vs. acceptable risk?
13. Do different brands have different tolerance levels for the same content signal (e.g. alcohol adjacent content OK for one brand, not another)?
14. What about creator history — does a past violation affect future scoring? How far back?

### System of record
15. Where does the approval decision land — your platform's CMS, the brand's portal, a Slack approval?
16. Who currently has final sign-off — platform side, brand side, both?
17. When a brand asks "why was this creator approved?" what's the answer today?

### Replication
18. Across 150 brands, how many share one safety template vs. need their own?
19. Onboarding a new brand — what do you need from them to configure their rules?
20. If you could template this, what's the per-brand setup time you'd commit to?

### Governance
21. Content categories that always require a human — regulated products, political adjacency, anything else?
22. When the model flags something ambiguous, who breaks the tie?
23. Kill switch — who has it? What happens to in-flight campaigns if it triggers?
