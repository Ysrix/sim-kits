# Interview guide — Agency RFP + franchise co-op compliance

**Domain expert:** Brad Webb
**GTM interviewer:** Titus Capilnean
**Time:** 45–60 min
**Goal:** Leave with enough to write a one-page scope memo. Run it like a real prospect discovery call.

## Framing for Brad (read verbatim)

"I'm going to run this like a prospect call. You're a franchisor CMO or a head of agency ops at a 200-location brand. I'm going to ask you about a workflow that keeps getting postponed. Answer as the buyer, not as Brad. I'll break character if I need to, but stay in role."

## The workflow

Co-op ad compliance. Franchisees submit local ads, the franchisor reviews them against brand guidelines before reimbursing. Today it's manual, inconsistent, and slow.

## Questions

### Pain
1. How often do you run this review? Volume per week?
2. Who does it today? What's their title, what do they cost?
3. What happens when it's late? Who complains?
4. Last time this broke, what went wrong?

### Current state
5. Where do submissions come from? Email, portal, shared drive?
6. What does an approval look like — a form, a Slack message, a line in a spreadsheet?
7. What are the actual brand guidelines? PDF, a wiki, tribal knowledge?
8. Reject rate today?

### Success
9. If this worked, what changes? Cost, speed, consistency, all three?
10. What's the minimum quality bar? (What would an auto-approval need to look like for you to trust it?)
11. What's the first thing that would make you kill the pilot?

### System of record
12. Where does the approval decision need to land? Reimbursement system, franchisee portal, GL?
13. Who currently has write access there?
14. What's the audit requirement — who asks to see decisions after the fact?

### Governance
15. What's non-negotiable — brand-safety categories, trademark use, anything legal-flagged?
16. What always needs a human?
17. What's the kill-switch scenario?
